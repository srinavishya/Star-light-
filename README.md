# ✦ Star Light

### Learn • Practise • Achieve

**Star Light** is a student-friendly educational website developed to support Tamil Nadu State Board Class 12 students in their learning and examination preparation.

It brings together subject-wise, chapter-based learning resources in one organised platform, helping students practise questions, understand concepts and revise important topics.

🌐 **Live Website:** [Visit Star Light](https://srinavishya.github.io/Star-light-/)

---

## 📚 Subjects Covered

Star Light provides learning resources for four major Class 12 subjects:

- 📘 **Mathematics**
- ⚛️ **Physics**
- 🧪 **Chemistry**
- 💻 **Computer Science**

Each subject is organised into chapters to make learning and navigation easier.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Chapter-wise Learning** – Explore individual chapters and their learning materials.
- **Book-back Questions** – Practise textbook-based questions.
- **Important Questions** – Focus on selected questions for revision and practice.
- **Worked Examples** – Understand concepts through step-by-step explanations and solutions.
- **Formula & Concept Sheets** – Revise important formulas and concepts.
- **My Bookmarks** – Save questions for convenient access and future revision.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make learning simple, organised and accessible for students.

Star Light aims to bring essential learning resources together in one place and encourage students to develop confidence through regular practice and better understanding.

We believe that every student can improve with the right resources, consistent effort and confidence.
