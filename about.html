About Star Light

Learn • Practise • Achieve

What is Star Light?

Star Light is a student-friendly learning platform designed for Tamil Nadu State Board Class 12 students. It brings learning resources, practice questions and solutions together in one place to make exam preparation easier and more organised.

What can you do with Star Light?

Book-back Questions

Practise textbook questions organised chapter by chapter and exercise by exercise.

Previous-year Questions

Prepare using verified public examination questions from previous years.

Important Questions

Practise selected questions organised according to their marks.

Worked Examples

Understand problems through clear, step-by-step solutions.

Formula Sheets

Revise important formulas with explanations and guidance on when to use them.

My Bookmarks

Save useful questions and return to them during revision.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make learning simple, organised and accessible. We want to encourage regular practice, improve understanding and help students build confidence in their studies.
