About Star Light

Star Light is a learning platform designed to help Tamil Nadu State Board Class 12 students prepare for their examinations. It provides chapter-wise textbook questions, previous-year public examination questions, important practice questions and worked examples with solutions. Our goal is to make learning easier, encourage regular practice and help students build confidence in their studies.
